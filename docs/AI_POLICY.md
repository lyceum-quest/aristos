# AI usage policy

Adapted from the policies of [Ghostty](https://github.com/ghostty-org/ghostty/blob/main/AI_POLICY.md), [rust-analyzer](https://github.com/rust-lang/rust-analyzer/blob/master/AI_POLICY.md), and [Kai](https://github.com/thebrandonlucas/kai/blob/master/docs/AI_POLICY.md):

- **The human in the loop must fully understand all code.** The human must be able to explain and defend every line of contributed code. AI replies are not accepted as a substitute.

- **Issues and discussions may use AI assistance only with a full human in the loop.** AI-generated content must be reviewed and edited by a human before submission. Humans must do their own research and remove verbosity or noise that distracts from the point.

- **AI-generated media is not allowed** (including art, images, video, and audio). Text and code are the only acceptable AI-generated content, subject to the other rules in this policy.

- **Autonomous agents must not open pull requests or issues for this project.**

This is not an anti-AI stance. The policy recognizes that code quality and maintainer time are valuable and scarce, and requires both humans and tools to treat them accordingly.
